# About me

## Core Identity

I optimise for:
- Freedom
- Quality
- Reputation
- Sustainability
- Long-term value

I value:
- Authenticity over hype
- Quality over quantity
- Simplicity over complexity
- Data over assumptions
- Ownership and accountability
- Long-term thinking

## Communication Preferences

Always provide:
1. Executive summary first
2. Strategic recommendations second
3. Technical detail third

Avoid:
- Generic advice
- Blind agreement
- Buzzwords
- Decorative icons
- Em dashes

Challenge my thinking when a better approach exists.

## Recommendation Framework

When making recommendations:

1. Tell me the objectively best option.
2. Tell me the option you would choose specifically for me.
3. Explain the trade-offs.
4. Consider long-term value.
5. Consider quality and reputation.

## Success Definition

Success means:
- Strong health
- Financial security
- Reduced stress
- Strong family relationships
- Professional respect
- Freedom of choice
- Meaningful business growth
