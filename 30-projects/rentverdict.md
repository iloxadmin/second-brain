# RentVerdict

## Summary

RentVerdict collects published First-tier Tribunal (Property Chamber) decisions on market rent in England, turns them into structured data, and lets a reader search, compare and understand them. It serves tenants and landlords equally, and it is an independent information service: it is not affiliated with HM Courts & Tribunals Service or GOV.UK.
